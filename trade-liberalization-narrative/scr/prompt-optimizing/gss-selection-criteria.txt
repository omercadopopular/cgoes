# Gold-Standard Sample (GSS) Selection Method – Summary

**Objective:**  
Select 8–12 documents from the WTO TPR corpus to serve as a gold-standard set for prompt tuning and evaluation of trade reform extraction and classification.

**Inputs:**
1. Country-level trade reform episodes (Salinas & Aksoy 2006; Sachs & Warner; Wacziarg & Welch)
2. User's `global_tpr_manifest.json` (list of available TPRs)

---

## Steps:

### 1. Cross-reference countries  
Identify countries listed in Salinas & Aksoy with documented reform episodes between 1980–2001.  
Retain only those for which a TPR document exists within ±5 years of the reform year.

### 2. Apply selection filters  
From the intersection, choose candidates that maximize:

- **Regional diversity** (e.g., Africa, Asia, Latin America)  
- **Reform type coverage** (tariff cuts, NTB removals, FTA-driven changes)  
- **Document variation** (older OCR-era and newer structured TPRs)  
- **Reform density** (include both low- and high-episode countries)

### 3. Score and rank candidates  
Assign a composite score to each candidate based on:

- Reform proximity to available TPR year  
- Number of available TPRs  
- Regional representation  
- Richness of reform episode tags

### 4. Select top-ranked 8–12 countries  
Choose final GSS to cover the full motive taxonomy and document formats needed for prompt tuning and benchmarking.
